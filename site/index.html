Financials

One private page for net worth, spending and where it's all headed.

Why I built it. I got tired of logging into every bank to answer "how are we doing?" Financials reads my own Google Sheets live in the browser and stores nothing.

  • Net worth at a glanceAssets, debt and cash on hand across every account.
  • Every month, every categorySpending month by month, compared with last year.
  • Where it's headedTFSA, RRSP and FHSA projected 25 years out.
  • Private by designData stays in your sheets. Nothing stored, nothing shared.
