Financials

One private page for a household's money: net worth, spending, every fund looked through to the companies, and where it's all headed.

Open the live demo no sign-in

Why I built it. I got tired of logging into every bank to answer "how are we doing?" Financials reads my own Google Sheets and bank statements, live and privately. The demo above runs the same app in your browser on made-up data.

What's inside

  • Overview and net worthAssets, debt and cash, how they moved, and what they're made of.
  • SpendingMonth by month, by category and merchant, with recurring payments found for you.
  • Credit cards and bank accountsEach on its own: e-Transfers, bills and big payments.
  • Cash flowAn income statement for the household, with every transaction.
  • Holdings, inside every fundEach ETF looked through to the companies, with its fees and price history.
  • Portfolio analysisSectors, countries, currencies, fee tiers, overlap and how the mix would have done.
  • Investment incomeEvery dividend and distribution, by month and by holding.
  • Contribution roomTFSA, RRSP and FHSA, counted from your transactions.
  • Projections and calculatorsTwenty-five years out, low to high case, and mortgage what-ifs.
